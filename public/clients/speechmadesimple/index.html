01 · The opportunity

The category is stuck in 2018.

What everyone else is doing

  • Slider heroes with cartoon palettes — coded for kids, not parents.
  • Stock photography that erodes trust on contact.
  • Schools buried as a location tile, not a program.
  • Fees hidden behind "funding".

What we'd do instead

  • Editorial typography on warm cream — feels grown-up.
  • Documentary photography in real homes and classrooms.
  • School partnerships as a flagship, not a footnote.
  • Transparent fees as a premium signal.
02 · The current site

One thing to keep, two to change.

Keep

The "popcorn" philosophy

The most human line on the site, buried in the hero. We let it lead: children pop at their own pace.

Change

Schools is a location, not a program

One of four location tiles. Jessica's 60+ school history deserves its own page, its own pricing, its own audience.

Add

A first-visit narrative

Worried parents arrive at 9pm. Nothing on the current site explains what an assessment actually looks like.

How it works

From first call to first session, no surprises.

Most families come to us worried. Here's exactly what happens — so you know what you're saying yes to.

01 · A first call

Tell us what you're noticing

Free 15-minute call. We'll tell you honestly whether an assessment is the right next step.

02 · Assessment

An hour or two, then a report

One or two sessions in your home or your child's setting. You leave with a written report.

03 · Therapy

Weekly or fortnightly

Short sessions with specific goals. Five-minute home-practice ideas — not a worksheet pile.

04 · Review

We talk about whether to keep going

Every 8–10 sessions we sit down together. Sometimes the right answer is to stop.

Common questions

What most parents ask first.

If your question isn't here, just ring or email.

How do I know if my child needs therapy at all?+
Often you can't be sure — that's what the free 15-minute call is for. Sometimes the honest answer is "wait six months and see".
Where do sessions happen?+
Usually in your home or your child's kindy or school. Moonee Valley and the surrounding inner-west. Telehealth where it suits the goal.
What does a session cost?+
45-minute sessions at a transparent flat rate, listed on the Fees page. No assessment loading, no admin fees.
Are you an NDIS provider?+
Not registered, but we work with self- and plan-managed plans.
Do I need a GP referral?+
No. A GP referral is only needed if you want a Medicare rebate via a Chronic Disease Management Plan.
Can my child's teacher be involved?+
Yes, and we recommend it. We share reports and brief classroom staff with your consent.
